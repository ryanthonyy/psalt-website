Support

How can we help?

Answers to common questions are below. For anything else, email support@thepsalt.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

Is Psalt really free?

Yes. Psalt is a free, non-profit project. There are no ads, no subscriptions and no paid features.

Which Bible translation does Psalt use?

The King James Version (KJV). Scripture is always quoted word for word, including in quizzes, verse cards and the verse of the day.

How do I change the text size, font or colors?

Tap Aa while reading, or the gear on the Home screen. You can change the text size, line spacing, font (including a high-legibility font for low vision), theme (light, dark, sepia or high contrast) and verse numbers.

Read-aloud sounds robotic. Can it sound more natural?

Read-aloud uses the voices on your phone. On iPhone, download a better one in Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices › English: choose a voice marked Enhanced or Premium. Then choose it in Psalt's settings, or leave Psalt on "Best available". Human-narrated audio is coming.

My daily reminder doesn't arrive.

Check that it's on in Psalt's settings under Daily, and that notifications are allowed for Psalt in your phone's settings (Settings › Notifications › Psalt on iPhone). Reminders are scheduled a month ahead each time you open Psalt, so open the app once if you haven't in a while.

How does the reading streak work?

A day counts when you read a chapter to the end, read or listen to Psalt Daily, or take a quiz. You can hide the streak in settings. Reading plans go at your own pace, so missing a day never loses your place.

Will I lose my medals and progress?

Your progress is saved on your phone. It stays when you update Psalt, but deleting the app deletes it. Accounts, which will let you keep your progress across phones, are planned.

How do you decide where places go on the map?

Locations come from OpenBible.info, which compiles over seventy modern scholarly sources. Psalt shows only the location scholars rank highest, colors each pin by how strongly they agree, and gives no pin to places whose location is unknown.

I found a mistake.

Thank you! Email support@thepsalt.com with the book, chapter and verse (or the quiz question), and what looks wrong.
