What's inside

Everything you need to read the Bible, and stay with it.

The full King James Bible

All 66 books, with a verse of the day, reading plans and text you can size, space and theme the way you like.

Listen as you read

Read-aloud highlights each verse as it's spoken and carries on into the next chapter. Human-narrated audio is on the way.

Every place on a map

Over a thousand places, with pins colored by how sure scholars are of each location. Tap a name in the text to see it.

Every era on a timeline

All 66 books across ten eras, from Creation to the early church, with an honest note on how firm each date is.

Quiz me

Chapter quizzes where every answer cites its verse. Earn bronze, silver and gold medals and collect verse cards.

Coming next

Watch the stories

Animated scenes narrated in the words of the King James text itself, never rewritten, starting with Creation and Psalm 23.
