Privacy policy

Your reading stays yours.

Effective November 10, 2026

In short: the Psalt app keeps everything on your phone. It has no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not sell or share your information with anyone.

The Psalt app

What the app stores, and where. Your settings (text size, font, theme, voice and speed), your reading progress, the last chapter you read, your quiz medals, points and verse cards, your reading streak and your reading plan are saved only on your phone. They are not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Daily reminder. The reminder is off unless you turn it on. If you do, Psalt asks your phone for permission and schedules the notifications on the phone itself. No notification service or server is involved, and you can turn it off at any time in Psalt's settings or your phone's settings.

Read-aloud. Read-aloud uses your phone's built-in voices, which work on the phone.

Maps. The map in the app is provided by your phone's map service (Apple Maps on iPhone, Google Maps on Android). Showing a map requests map images from that service, which is covered by its own privacy policy. Psalt does not use your location.

Sharing. When you choose to share a verse or a medal, Psalt opens your phone's share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose to.

Human-narrated audio (coming). When we add recorded Bible narration, the audio will stream from Faith Comes By Hearing's Bible Brain service. As with any audio you stream, their servers receive basic technical information such as your IP address. We will update this policy when that feature arrives.

This website

  • Hosting. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts and maps. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and map data from OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap. Your browser contacts those services to fetch them.
  • No cookies or analytics. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Beta sign-up. If you ask to join the beta, we keep your email address and the type of phone you use only to send you the invitation and beta news. Ask us any time and we'll delete them.

Children

Psalt is made for readers of all ages. The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If we change how Psalt handles information, for example when accounts or recorded narration are added, we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect.
